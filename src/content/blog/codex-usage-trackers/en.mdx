I just wanted something to show me my Codex usage. I ended up trying
<SourceLink href="https://www.openusage.ai/">OpenUsage</SourceLink>,
<SourceLink href="https://github.com/vinzdg/codenotch">CodeNotch</SourceLink>, and
<SourceLink href="https://codexbar.app/">CodexBar</SourceLink>.

For now, I'm keeping CodexBar. I'm picky about software, even a small
utility like this.

## OpenUsage

OpenUsage was a good experience overall, but the interface didn't feel quite
polished. During testing, the interface flickered a lot and kept redrawing.
Icons also changed size when I interacted with the app.

<Figure
  src={openUsagePanel}
  darkSrc={openUsagePanelDark}
  alt="OpenUsage showing 84% of my weekly Codex allowance left"

I liked having my usage in the menu bar, but the flickering and resizing
were distracting. The design itself is pretty boring in a good way, and
there's plenty to customize.

One feature I really like is copying a usage image with a button and pasting
it wherever I need it. These are the light and dark versions from OpenUsage.

## CodeNotch

CodeNotch looks amazing on the right side of the screen. Visually, it was my
favorite of the three. The animation is great, too.

I still wanted more features, though. I liked the look enough that I might
come back to it, but it wasn't my pick this time.

## CodexBar

CodexBar ticks all my boxes. It feels more like a native Apple app, it's
fast, and it does what I need. With a bit of customization, it looks just
the way I want it. Its
<SourceLink href="https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar#features">display settings</SourceLink>
cover icons, labels, usage bars, and how reset times appear. I appreciate being
able to adjust those details.

It also looks well maintained to me. The
<SourceLink href="https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar/releases">release history</SourceLink>
gave me more confidence in it.

<Figure
  src={codexBarPanel}
  darkSrc={codexBarPanelDark}
  alt="CodexBar showing 84% of my weekly Codex allowance left and two available reset credits"
  caption="My CodexBar setup, showing the remaining weekly allowance and reset credits."

When I checked on September 11, 2026,
<SourceLink href="https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar">CodexBar</SourceLink>
had about 21.2k GitHub stars, compared with about 4.1k for
<SourceLink href="https://github.com/robinebers/openusage">OpenUsage</SourceLink>
and 1.4k for
<SourceLink href="https://github.com/vinzdg/codenotch">CodeNotch</SourceLink>.
The larger following was reassuring. I chose it because it worked well for me.

I might revisit these apps. For now, I needed something to show me my Codex
usage, and CodexBar does that really well for me.
