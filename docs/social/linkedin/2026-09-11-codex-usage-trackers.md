## Post

I found a Codex usage tracker I like after trying OpenUsage, CodeNotch, and CodexBar.

OpenUsage is pretty boring in a good way, with plenty to customize. I liked being able to copy and paste an image of my usage with a button. The flickering and icons changing size after interactions still bothered me.

CodeNotch looks amazing on the right side of the screen, and the animation is great. My favorite visually, though I still wanted more features.

CodexBar is the one I'm keeping. It feels more like a native Apple app, it's fast, and with a bit of customization it looks just the way I want it.

I'm picky about software, even a usage indicator in my menu bar.
